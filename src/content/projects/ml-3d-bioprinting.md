Reduced material waste and calibration time by training an XGBoost regression model to predict
optimal 3D (bio)printing parameters from design images, deploying the algorithm into a standalone
GUI to eliminate manual calibration.

Related publication: *Machine learning-enabled optimization of extrusion-based 3D printing* (Methods, Elsevier).
